01

Usual

Coffee bar with three branches in Cebu City

Concept project

The brief

Office workers and students on short breaks want their drink waiting when they walk in. Usual needed a name, a look and a site that takes the order before they leave their desk.

What we did

We kept the look plain so the drinks carry it: a warm-white page, near-black type, daylight photographs, and one stamp-pad blue used only for ordering and the loyalty stamps.

What we made

Identity
The name, the wordmark, the colour system and the type, set in Hanken Grotesk
Website
Menu, order ahead, rewards, branches, account and bag, plus the company and legal pages
Order ahead
Build a drink, pick a branch and a pickup time, see your name on the cup and collect a stamp

02

Bayline

Car wash and detailing in Subangdaku, Mandaue City

Concept project

The brief

Nobody likes driving over to find six cars ahead. Bayline needed a site that shows the wait before you leave home and prices every wash by the size of your vehicle.

What we did

Real car-wash photographs, white catalogue sections, one graphite band for the live bay board, and one red only for the things that can’t wait. Saira, with its width axis, sets prices and plates.

What we made

Identity
The name, the wordmark, the colour system and the type, set in Saira
Booking site
Prices by vehicle, monthly wash packs, the line, location, account and basket, plus the company and legal pages
The line
Join by plate number and watch the bays, or book a time and choose GCash, Maya or card

Enamel

Family dental clinic in Guadalupe, Cebu City

Concept project

The brief

People put off the dentist when they can’t see what it will cost. Enamel needed a calm site with every starting price up front and the HMO paperwork explained before the first visit.

What we did

A cool porcelain page, deep spruce text and one soft apricot for the tooth you pick. Newsreader for headings and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything you read or tap.

What we made

Identity
The name, the mark, the colour system and two typefaces
Website
Treatments and prices, new patients, the team, booking and account, plus the company and legal pages
Tooth chart
Pick a tooth to see its usual treatments and prices, then book a dentist and a time in five steps
