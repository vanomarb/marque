Accessibility

How this site is built so that everyone can use it, and where it falls short.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to read the site, price a brief and send it, whatever they use to browse. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we did

  • Text and controls meet the AA contrast ratios: ink on paper is 16.7 to 1, the grey text is 6.9 to 1.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, in the order you see it, with a visible focus outline. A “Skip to content” link comes first.
  • The project builder uses real checkboxes, radio buttons and buttons, and announces each new estimate to screen readers.
  • Form errors are written out next to the field, not shown by colour alone, and focus moves to the first one.
  • Pages work from 360 pixels wide, and text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
  • With reduced motion set on your device, nothing on the site moves.
  • Every screenshot of our work has a text description.

Known limits

The screenshots on the Work page are pictures of other sites: their own text is described, not reproduced. The builder needs JavaScript to add up prices; without it, each row still shows its starting price. The contact, sign-in and newsletter forms also need JavaScript, so without it they stay switched off and nothing you type leaves the page.

This is a demonstration site

Marque is a concept, so the site is not monitored for feedback. The checks above were made in a current browser at desktop, tablet and phone widths and with a keyboard; the reduced-motion rules are checked by the site’s tests.
