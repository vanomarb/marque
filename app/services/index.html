How the prices work

Every price on this page is a starting price in US dollars. After the first call you get one fixed price for your job, in writing, with what’s included and the dates.

New clients get 15% off each one-time service on their first project, rounded to the dollar in your favour and taken off these regular prices. Monthly work isn’t discounted. One first-project discount per client.

Every one-time service is ready in 1 to 3 days, counted from the day you approve the quote (for a website, from the day your text and photos are in; for photo and video, from the day of the shoot). If we deliver after day 3, $5 comes off the final invoice for each day late, up to the price of that service.

One-time work is paid half when you approve the quote and half on delivery. Monthly work is billed at the start of each month: social media and ads stop with 30 days’ notice, and Managed hosting cancels any time.

Questions owners ask

Why do the prices say “from”?

Because no two businesses need exactly the same thing. The starting price covers what’s listed under each service. After the first call we send one fixed price for your job, in writing.

Who gets the first-project discount?

New clients, on their first one-time project with us: 15% off each of the identity, website, shoots and print in it, rounded to the dollar in your favour. Monthly work stays at the regular price, and it’s one discount per client.

Can we get just a logo?

No. Brand identity starts at $390 and includes the logo files, colours, type and a short guidelines PDF, so the logo works on a sign or a cup. We don’t sell the logo on its own.

Who owns the files when we’re done?

You do, once the project is paid: the logo files, the guidelines and the website’s code. On Managed hosting we keep the code running for you, and it’s yours to take whenever you leave.

Is three days really enough?

For a small business, yes. The brief and the quote settle what’s needed before we start, and one team does the design and the build. A website’s days start once your text and photos are in, and a shoot’s on the day of the shoot. If we’re late, $5 comes off your invoice for each day after day 3, up to the price of that service; days we spend waiting for your replies or approvals don’t count.

Do you sell domains?

No. Domain prices vary too much to bundle, so we don’t buy, sell or renew them. On Managed hosting your site can stay on a free Marque subdomain, or you buy a domain from any registrar and we connect it for you.

Can I leave Managed hosting?

Yes, any time. The site stays up until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and we transfer the code to you so you can host it anywhere.

What happens on handover?

We transfer the code repository to you with a short written guide to deploying it. Hosting and the domain are then yours to set up and pay for. There’s no monthly fee from us.

Is the ad spend part of ads management?

No. The monthly fee pays for running the ads. What Meta and Google charge for the ads themselves is billed to your card directly, at a budget you set.

How many rounds of changes do we get?

Two rounds on the direction you pick, for identity, website and print work. More are fine; we price them before we start.

How do we pay?

By card or bank transfer, against an invoice. Half when you approve the quote, half on delivery; monthly work at the start of each month.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. Briefs, calls and reviews happen online, so it works the same wherever you are. Shoots are done by a vetted photographer near your business.
