How the prices work

Every price on this page is a starting price in Philippine pesos. After the first call you get one fixed price for your job, in writing, with what’s included and a schedule.

One-time work is paid half when you approve the quote and half at hand-over. Monthly work is billed at the start of each month.

Questions owners ask

Why do the prices say “from”?

Because no two cafés need exactly the same thing. The starting price covers what’s listed under each service. After the first call we send one fixed price for your job, in writing.

Can we get just a logo?

Yes. Brand identity is the logo plus the colours, type and guidelines that make it usable on a sign or a cup. If you only need the logo file, say so in your brief and we’ll price that.

Who owns the files when we’re done?

You do, once the project is paid. You get the logo source files, the font licences, the website and every login, in your business’s name.

Is the ad spend part of ads management?

No. The monthly fee pays for running the ads. What Meta and Google charge for the ads themselves is billed to your card directly, at a budget you set.

How many rounds of changes do we get?

Two rounds on the direction you pick, for identity, website and print work. More are fine; we price them before we start.

How do we pay?

By bank transfer, GCash or Maya, against an invoice. Half when you approve the quote, half at hand-over; monthly work at the start of each month.

Do you work outside Cebu?

Shoots and working sessions happen in person, so those are in Metro Cebu. Websites, social media and ads can be done for a business anywhere in the Philippines.
