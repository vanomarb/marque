Prices and estimates

Every price is a starting price in Philippine pesos. The project builder adds up starting prices; it is an estimate, not a quote or an offer. A studio would confirm one fixed price in writing after the first call, with what’s included and a schedule.

How the studio would work with you

Once a project is paid in full, you own the final logo files, designs, photographs and website made for you, and every account is set up in your business’s name. The studio may show the finished work on its own site unless you ask it not to.
