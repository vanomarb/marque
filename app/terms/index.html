Prices and estimates

Every price is a starting price in US dollars. The project builder adds up starting prices; it is an estimate, not a quote or an offer. We confirm one fixed price in writing after the first call, with what’s included and the dates.

First-project discount

New clients get 15% off each one-time service on their first project with Marque, taken off the regular prices shown on the site and rounded to the dollar in your favour. Monthly work isn’t discounted. One first-project discount per client.

Delivery dates and the late-day credit

Every one-time service (brand identity, website, photo and video, and print design) is ready in 1 to 3 days. The days count from the day you approve the quote; for a website, from the day we have your text and photos; for photo and video, from the day of the shoot. Days we spend waiting for your replies or approvals don’t count.

If we deliver a service after day 3, we take $5 off the final invoice for every day late, up to the price of that service.

How we work with you

For one-time work, half the fixed price is paid when you approve the quote and half on delivery. Monthly work is billed at the start of each month. Social media and ads management can be stopped with 30 days’ notice; Managed hosting can be cancelled at any time and runs to the end of the month already paid. Ad spend is billed by the ad platforms directly. Managed hosting includes hosting on a free Marque subdomain or a domain you own, daily backups, uptime monitoring, security updates, SEO setup and up to an hour of edits a month.

We aim to reply to every message within a day, to send a quote within a day of a complete brief and to show the first design directions on day 1. These are targets, not guarantees.

Once a project is paid in full, you own the final logo files, designs, photographs and the website’s code made for you. On Managed hosting we host that code and transfer it to you if you cancel; on a handed-over site the code is transferred on delivery, and hosting is yours to arrange. Domains aren’t part of any service: we don’t buy, sell or renew them, and any domain you use is yours. We may show the finished work on our own site unless you ask us not to.
