02

How a project runs

A brand identity and a six-page website, done together, take 1 to 3 days once you approve the quote and send your text and photos. Day 3 is the promise: if we’re late, $5 comes off for each day. This is what happens in those days.

  1. 1

    Brief

    You send the brief from the builder, or tell us on a call what you’re opening, for whom and by when. We ask about your customers, your competitors on the same street and what has to be ready on opening day.

    Before day 1

  2. 2

    Quote

    One fixed price, the dates and a list of what’s included, in writing, within a day of the brief. When you approve it and pay half, the dates are yours.

    Before day 1

  3. 3

    Design

    Two directions for the identity on day 1, shown on mock-ups of your sign, cup or receipt. You pick one; two rounds of changes follow. The website’s pages are designed on top of it.

    Day 1

  4. 4

    Build

    We build the site from your text and photos and prepare the print files. You test everything on your own phone, on a private preview link.

    Days 2 to 3

  5. 5

    Launch

    On Managed hosting, $39 a month, the site goes live on a free Marque subdomain or a domain you own, with SSL, backups and SEO setup in place. Handed over, we transfer the code with a short deploy guide. Either way you get the logo files and guidelines, and monthly social and ads can start the next day.

    By day 3

What we don’t do

We don’t print or build signs. We work with the printer and sign maker you choose, send them ready files and check their proofs before the run.

We don’t run influencer campaigns or buy followers, and we don’t promise numbers we can’t control. We tell you what ran, what it cost and what we’d change.
