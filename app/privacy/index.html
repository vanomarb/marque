Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information, following the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This is a demonstration site

Nothing you enter on this site is sent to us or to anyone else. The project builder, the sign-in, the contact form and the newsletter work only inside your browser. There are no accounts on a server, no analytics and no advertising.

What this site keeps, and where

Two items are kept in your browser’s local storage, on your device only, and only after you use the feature that needs them:

marque.account
Your sign-in: the first name and the email address or mobile number you entered, so the site can greet you and show your briefs.
marque.briefs
Your saved briefs: the services, options and date of each brief you save from the builder.

Signing out removes the first item. You can remove briefs one by one under Your briefs, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings. The Cookie Policy lists the same items.

What a real project would involve

Working with a studio, you would share your name, your business’s name, contact details, and material about your business such as photos, menus and price lists. A studio would use them only to do the work you asked for, keep them only as long as the project and the law require, and share them only with the providers that work needs, such as a printer or a web host, with your agreement.

The law that applies

Personal information in the Philippines is protected by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission. This policy follows them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access your information, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Security

The site loads no third-party scripts, and its fonts and icons are served from the site itself. What it keeps never leaves your device.

Contact

To reach the studio’s Data Protection Officer, use the contact form and write “Privacy” in the message. As this is a demonstration site, messages are not sent.
