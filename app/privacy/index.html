Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information, following the data protection law where you live.

Last updated 20 November 2026

What isn’t connected yet

Sign-in, saved briefs, the contact form and the newsletter work only inside your browser for now. Nothing you enter on this site is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts on a server, no analytics and no advertising. When that changes, this policy will say what we collect and why before it happens.

What this site keeps, and where

Two items are kept in your browser’s local storage, on your device only, and only after you use the feature that needs them:

marque.account
Your sign-in: the email address you entered, and your first name if you gave one, so the site can greet you and show your briefs.
marque.briefs
Your saved briefs: the services, options and date of each brief you save from the builder.

Signing out removes the first item. You can remove briefs one by one under Your briefs, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings. The Cookie Policy lists the same items.

When you work with us

To do a project, we ask for your name, your business’s name, your email address, and material about your business such as photos, menus and price lists. We use them only to do the work you asked for, keep them only as long as the project and the law require, and share them only with the providers the work needs, such as a printer, a photographer or a web host, with your agreement.

The law that applies

Your information is protected by the data protection law where you live, such as the GDPR in the European Union. This policy follows the law that gives you the most protection.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access your information, to have it corrected or erased, to object, and to data portability. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Security

The site loads no third-party scripts, and its fonts and icons are served from the site itself. What it keeps never leaves your device.

Contact

To reach us about privacy, use the contact form and write “Privacy” in the message. Sending isn’t connected yet, so for now the form keeps your message on the page rather than delivering it.
