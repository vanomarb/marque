04

Two rules we keep

First project, 15% off
New clients get 15% off each one-time service on their first project, rounded to the dollar in your favour. A service costs the same first-project price alone or in any mix. Monthly work isn’t discounted. One per client.
Ready by day 3, or $5 off
Every one-time service is ready in 1 to 3 days, counted from the day you approve the quote (for a website, from the day your text and photos are in; for photo and video, from the day of the shoot). If we deliver after day 3, $5 comes off the final invoice for each day late, up to the price of that service. Days we spend waiting for your replies or approvals don’t count.

05

Billing questions

How do we pay?

By card or bank transfer, against an invoice. One-time work is paid half when you approve the quote and half on delivery. Monthly work is billed at the start of each month.

Who gets the first-project discount?

New clients, on their first one-time project with us: 15% off each of the identity, website, shoots and print in it, rounded to the dollar in your favour. Monthly work stays at the regular price, and it’s one discount per client.

What happens if you deliver late?

For each day after day 3, $5 comes off the final invoice, up to the price of that service. The days count from the day you approve the quote; for a website, from the day your text and photos are in; for photo and video, from the day of the shoot. Days we spend waiting for your replies or approvals don’t count.

Can I cancel Managed hosting?

Yes, any time. The site stays up until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and we transfer the code to you so you can host it anywhere.

What about the domain?

It stays yours: we don’t buy, sell or renew them. Use the free Marque subdomain, or buy a domain from any registrar and we connect it for you.

How do I stop social media or ads?

Give us 30 days’ notice, any month. The ad spend is never ours to stop: Meta and Google bill it to your card at a budget you set.
