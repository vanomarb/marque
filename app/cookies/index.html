Cookie Policy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It keeps two essential items in your browser’s local storage, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

Last updated 18 November 2026

What the site keeps

There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising here. Two items are kept in your browser’s local storage, and only when you use the feature that needs them:

marque.account
Your first name and the email address or mobile number you signed in with. Set when you sign in; removed when you sign out.
marque.briefs
The briefs you save from the project builder: services, options and the date saved. Set when you press Save brief; remove each one under Your briefs.

Both are essential: without them, the sign-in and Your briefs can’t work. They never leave your device, and nothing reads them but this site in your browser.

No tracking

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts. The fonts and icons are part of the site.

Clearing what’s kept

Sign out to remove your sign-in, remove briefs under Your briefs, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings. If your browser blocks storage, the site still works; it just won’t remember you or your briefs.

This is a demonstration site

Nothing you enter here is sent anywhere. The Privacy Policy explains what a real project would involve.
